---
title: Market Terms
description: A concise glossary of prediction-market and order-book terms
---

Use this glossary when a term in the market page, order panel, or portfolio is unfamiliar.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Event** | A container for one or more related markets |
| **Market** | A tradable prediction question |
| **Outcome** | A side of the market, such as Yes, No, Up, Down, or a team |
| **Share** | A unit of an outcome position that settles to $1.00 if it wins |
| **Order book** | Open buy and sell orders grouped by price |
| **Bid** | A price a buyer is offering |
| **Ask** | A price a seller is offering |
| **Spread** | The difference between the best ask and best bid |
| **Midpoint** | The average of the best bid and best ask |
| **Liquidity** | The amount available to trade near the current price |
| **Slippage** | The difference between the expected and average execution price |
| **Market order** | An order designed to execute immediately against available liquidity |
| **Limit order** | An order that fills only at a chosen price or better |
| **Resolution** | The final outcome determined from the market rules and oracle process |
| **Dispute window** | The period when a proposed result may be challenged |
| **Deposit Wallet** | The trading wallet used for balances, approvals, and onchain actions |

<Callout type="info">
  “Price” may refer to a displayed midpoint or an executable bid or ask. Review the order
  estimate to see the price your trade is expected to receive.
</Callout>
