---
title: Canceling Orders
description: Remove unfilled limit orders from the order book
---

import { Steps, Step } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps'

Only open, unfilled Limit quantity can be canceled. A fill that already occurred remains
part of your position and trade history.

## Cancel one order

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open your orders

    Go to **Portfolio → Open Orders**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Find the order

    Locate the active order by market, outcome, side, or price.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm cancellation

    Select **Cancel** on the order row and wait for its status to update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel all orders

If the action is available for your account, select **Cancel all** in the Open Orders tab
to remove every active order for the connected wallet.

<Callout type="info">
  An order can fill while your cancellation is being processed. Check the final order
  status and your position before submitting a replacement.
</Callout>
