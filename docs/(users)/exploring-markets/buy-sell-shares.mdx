---
title: Buy & Sell Shares
description: Open a position, reduce it, or exit before resolution
---

import { Steps, Step } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps'

Buying shares opens or increases a position. Selling reduces an existing position and
returns the trade proceeds to your available balance after the fill settles.

## Before you trade

- Read the market rules and resolution source.
- Check the best bid, best ask, spread, and available size.
- Confirm that your balance covers the order and estimated fee.

## Buy shares

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose an outcome

    Open the market and select the outcome you expect to win.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Build the order

    Choose Market or Limit, enter the amount or number of shares, and review the
    estimated price, total, and fee.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign and monitor

    Submit the order. Filled shares appear in your portfolio; unfilled Limit quantity
    appears under Open Orders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell shares

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Select Sell

    Open the market, choose the outcome you hold, and switch the order panel to **Sell**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose execution

    Use Market for immediate execution against bids, or Limit to set your minimum price.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review and sign

    Confirm the shares, estimated proceeds, and fee. Monitor open quantity in
    **Portfolio → Open Orders**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  You cannot sell more shares than your available position. Shares committed to an open
  sell order may be unavailable until that order fills or is canceled.
</Callout>

You can sell before resolution or hold a winning position through settlement and claim
its payout.
