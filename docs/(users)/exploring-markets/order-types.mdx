Every order specifies an outcome, side, quantity, and price. The order type controls
whether you prioritize execution now or wait for a specific price.

## Compare order types

| | Market | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Execution** | Immediately against available orders | Only at your price or better |
| **Price control** | Final average depends on book depth | You set the maximum buy or minimum sell price |
| **Unfilled amount** | Canceled according to FAK/FOK preference | Remains open until filled or canceled |
| **Best for** | Speed | Price control |

## Market orders

A Market order trades against the best available prices. Large orders may fill across
multiple price levels, so always review the estimate before signing.

In **Settings → Trading**, <SiteName /> supports two execution preferences:

- **FAK (Fill and Kill):** fill the amount available now and cancel the remainder.
- **FOK (Fill or Kill):** fill the entire order immediately or cancel it entirely.

## Limit orders

A Limit order fills only at your chosen price or better. It can fill partially as matching
orders arrive. The remaining quantity stays in **Portfolio → Open Orders** until it fills
or you cancel it.

<Callout type="info">
  A market-style order is submitted with execution constraints designed to trade
  immediately. It still depends on available order-book liquidity.
</Callout>
