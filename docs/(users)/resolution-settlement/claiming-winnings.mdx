---
title: Claiming Winnings
description: Redeem winning shares after a market reaches final resolution
---

import { Steps, Step } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps'

Once a result is final, each winning share can be redeemed for $1.00. Claims are available
from your portfolio and, when shown, from the resolved market's order panel.

## Claim a position

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Find claimable markets

    Open **Portfolio** and look for the winnings card, or open the resolved market.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review the claim

    Confirm the markets and positions included in the request.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign

    Select **Claim** or **Claim winnings**, sign the request, and wait for confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check your balance

    After confirmation, the redeemed amount appears in your available trading balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payout example

| | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| 100 shares bought at $0.35 | $35 cost |
| Winning redemption | $100 |
| **Gross gain before fees** | **$65** |

<Callout type="info">
  A winning position is not claimable while the market is still proposed, disputed, or
  under final review.
</Callout>

If the claim fails after final resolution, record the market link, wallet address, error
message, and transaction hash before contacting support.
