The Direct Resolution Oracle (DRO) records the final outcome of a market onchain. An
approved resolver for <SiteName /> checks the published rules and resolution source,
then submits the official result.

Community reports help surface the expected result, but they do not settle the market by
themselves. The approved resolver always makes the final decision.

## Read the resolution rules first

Every market defines:

- **Resolution criteria:** what must happen for each outcome to win.
- **Resolution source:** where the result must be verified.
- **Timing and edge cases:** which date, timezone, threshold, cancellation rule, or other
  condition applies.

<Callout type="warning" title="Wait until the answer is verifiable">
  A report submitted too early or against the written rules can lose the entire 300 USDC bond. Market price, public
  opinion, and a similar market elsewhere do not determine the official result.
</Callout>

## How resolution works

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### The result becomes verifiable

    The event finishes or the condition in the market rules is met.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Users may report the outcome

    When Resolution Rewards is active, eligible users can lock the displayed bond and report **YES** or **NO**.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### The site submits the official result

    An approved resolver verifies the rules and source. User reports are signals; they cannot force the result.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Claims become available

    Winning shares redeem for **$1.00** each. Resolution bonds and rewards are claimed separately from
    [Settings → Rewards](/settings/rewards).

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resolution Rewards

The proposal modal shows the exact bond and reward before you sign. With the current
defaults, the bond is **300 USDC**, while the reward is normally **$4.00** for a standard
market or **$1.60 per question** in a multi-market event.

| Official result                                               | Your bond                         | Reward                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Your active report is correct                                 | Returned in full                  | The displayed market reward |
| Your report is incorrect                                      | Forfeited to the Security Reserve | None                        |
| The result is unknown, 50/50, canceled, or emergency-resolved | Returned in full                  | None                        |

<Callout type="info" title="The site does not profit from a wrong report">
  A forfeited bond never goes to <SiteName /> or its resolver. It goes to the separate Security Reserve used for
  security and future improvements to the rewards system.
</Callout>

### Participation limits

- Each DepositWallet can report only once per market for the market's entire lifetime.
- Only one active **YES** report and one active **NO** report can occupy the market at a time.
- Reporting both sides through different wallets is not risk-free: after a YES or NO result,
  one 300 USDC bond loses, while the correct side receives only its bond plus the small
  displayed reward.
- Reports cannot change sides.

### If the market remains unresolved

After 48 hours, the reporter may request the bond back. The request immediately gives up
the reward, remains exposed to the official result for another 24 hours, and cannot be
canceled. If a result arrives during that delay, a correct report receives only its bond
back and a wrong report loses it. Otherwise, the slot reopens and only the originating
DepositWallet can claim the bond.

## Security Reserve

The Security Reserve is held separately and may be used for security, exceptional
reimbursements, and future improvements to Resolution Rewards.

## After final resolution

- Claim winning positions from the resolved market or your portfolio.
- Claim a returned bond and any earned resolution reward from [Settings → Rewards](/settings/rewards).
- Losing shares redeem for **$0.00**.
