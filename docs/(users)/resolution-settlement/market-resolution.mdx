Resolution determines which outcome won. The market rules define the criteria and source;
the oracle workflow proposes, reviews, and finalizes the result.

## Resolution stages

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Trading ends** | The event reaches the time or condition defined in the rules |
| **Outcome proposed** | The oracle receives an initial result |
| **Dispute window / review** | The result may be challenged or receive additional review |
| **Final** | The winning outcome is fixed and eligible positions become redeemable |

On <SiteName />, these stages appear in the timeline on the market page. If a dispute or
final review applies, its status and available action appear there as well.

## Payout after resolution

- Each winning share redeems for **$1.00**.
- Each losing share redeems for **$0.00**.
- Your claim becomes available only after the result is final.

<Callout type="warning" title="The rules control the result">
  Resolve a market from its written criteria and named source—not from the title, common
  interpretation, or an outcome on another platform.
</Callout>

## What to do

1. Read the resolution rules before trading.
2. Monitor the market timeline after trading ends.
3. If the result is disputed, wait for the final review.
4. Claim the winning position when the claim action appears.
