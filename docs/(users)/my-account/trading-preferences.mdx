---
title: Trading Preferences
description: Choose how unfilled market-order quantity is handled
---

Open **Settings → Trading** to select the execution mode applied to future Market orders.

| Mode | Behavior | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FAK (Fill and Kill)** | Fill available quantity immediately and cancel the remainder | A partial fill is acceptable |
| **FOK (Fill or Kill)** | Fill the complete quantity immediately or cancel the order | You require the full size |

The preference does not change the behavior of resting Limit orders.

<Callout type="info">
  Both modes depend on available liquidity at submission time. Review the order estimate
  immediately before signing.
</Callout>
