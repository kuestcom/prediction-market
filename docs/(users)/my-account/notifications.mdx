---
title: Notifications
description: Control the trading and resolution alerts available in your deployment
---

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Each toggle is saved when you change it.

## Available preferences

| Preference | Channel |
| --- | --- |
| Order fills | In-app |
| Market resolutions | Email |
| Market resolutions | In-app |
| Hide fills smaller than 1 share | In-app filter |

Your operator may enable a different set of channels. Browser, device, and email-provider
settings can also affect delivery.

<Callout type="info">
  Notifications are a convenience, not a substitute for monitoring open orders,
  resolution deadlines, or dispute windows.
</Callout>
