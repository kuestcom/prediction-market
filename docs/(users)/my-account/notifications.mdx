---
title: Notifications
description: Control the trading and resolution alerts available in your deployment
---

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Each toggle is saved when you change it.

## Available preferences

| Preference                      | Channel                  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Followed trader activity        | Browser or installed app |
| Order fills                     | In-app                   |
| Market resolutions              | Email                    |
| Market resolutions              | In-app                   |
| Hide fills smaller than 1 share | In-app filter            |

## Followed trader activity

Turn on **Followed trader activity** to receive trade alerts when people you follow trade on the site. You can also
open **Manage followed traders** from this page to review who you follow or unfollow someone. Unfollowing a trader
also stops their trade alerts.

Your browser or device may ask for permission before these alerts can appear. On iPhone or iPad, you may need to add
the site to your Home Screen first.

Your operator may enable a different set of channels. Browser, device, and email-provider
settings can also affect delivery.

<Callout type="info">
  Notifications are a convenience, not a substitute for monitoring open orders, resolution deadlines, or dispute
  windows.
</Callout>
