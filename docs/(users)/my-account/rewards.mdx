Open **[Settings → Rewards](/settings/rewards)** to view affiliate earnings and rewards earned by reporting
market outcomes. Each program has its own available balance, lifetime earnings, and
**Claim** action.

## Affiliate rewards

Share your affiliate link or create an embeddable market widget. When an attributed
user trades, your configured share of eligible trading fees is credited as affiliate
rewards.

|                        |             Current value |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------: |
| Share of eligible fees | <AffiliateShareDisplay /> |

The affiliate card shows referrals, attributed volume, the current fee share, and recent
referrals. Commission is calculated from eligible trading fees, not directly from raw
trading volume.

## Resolution rewards

Eligible users can report the verifiable outcome of a market from its resolution modal.
The report requires the bond shown before confirmation, and each DepositWallet can
submit only once per market.

- A correct report returns the bond and credits the configured market reward.
- An incorrect report forfeits the bond.
- Claimable bond and reward balances remain available until the wallet claims them.

The resolution activity card shows bond at risk, correct and incorrect reports, accuracy,
and recent proposals. Select a proposal to return to its market.

See [DRO Market Resolution](/docs/resolution-settlement/market-resolution) for the full
reporting, withdrawal, and Security Reserve rules.

## Earnings history and claims

Use the chart to compare affiliate commissions and resolution rewards from the last 30
days. When either balance becomes available, select **Claim** in the corresponding card
and sign the wallet request.

<Callout type="info">
  Onchain settlement and indexed activity can update at different times. A completed claim may take a short time to
  appear in the activity view.
</Callout>
