---
title: Public Profile
description: See what other traders can view about your account
---

Your public profile is available at **/@username** and can be opened from
**Settings → View Public Profile**.

## Public information

Depending on the deployment and your visibility settings, the page can show:

- username, avatar, and join date;
- portfolio-performance summaries;
- public positions;
- recent public activity.

<Callout type="warning">
  Public blockchain activity may still be associated with your wallet even when a profile
  field or platform view is hidden.
</Callout>

Use [Profile Settings](/docs/my-account/profile-settings) to update your identity and
preview the result.
