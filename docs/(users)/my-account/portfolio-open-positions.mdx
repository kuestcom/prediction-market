title: Portfolio & Open Positions
description: Track holdings, working orders, completed activity, and claimable payouts
---

Your portfolio is the central view for trading activity associated with the connected
wallet.

## Portfolio tabs

| Tab             | What it shows                                              |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Positions**   | Current holdings and mark-to-market value                  |
| **Open Orders** | Unfilled Limit orders that can still fill or be canceled   |
| **History**     | Completed trading and wallet activity with export controls |

## Claimable winnings

When at least one winning position becomes redeemable, a winnings card appears in the
portfolio. Review the included markets, select **Claim**, sign the request, and wait for
your available balance to update.

## Resolved positions

A resolved losing position can remain visible until its shares are claimed or consumed.
It may show **0** current value and a negative PnL while the tokens remain in the wallet.

## Portfolio metrics

- **Past day**: PnL change over the last 24 hours.
- **Biggest Win**: highest realized PnL from a closed position.

<Callout type="info">
  Portfolio values can change with current market prices. A displayed position value is an estimate until you sell or
  redeem the shares.
</Callout>

Use **Open Orders** to cancel resting orders and **History** to verify completed fills,
fees, and exports.
