Your portfolio is the central view for trading activity associated with the connected
wallet.

## Portfolio tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Positions** | Current holdings and mark-to-market value |
| **Open Orders** | Unfilled Limit orders that can still fill or be canceled |
| **History** | Completed trading and wallet activity with export controls |

## Claimable winnings

When at least one winning position becomes redeemable, a winnings card appears in the
portfolio. Review the included markets, select **Claim**, sign the request, and wait for
your available balance to update.

<Callout type="info">
  Portfolio values can change with current market prices. A displayed position value is
  an estimate until you sell or redeem the shares.
</Callout>

Use **Open Orders** to cancel resting orders and **History** to verify completed fills,
fees, and exports.
