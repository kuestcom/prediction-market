Open **Settings → Affiliate** to copy your referral link and monitor referred activity.

## Dashboard metrics

- referral link and current commission rate;
- total referrals and active traders;
- attributed trading volume;
- earned and claimable affiliate fees.

## How commission works

Commission is paid from the operator fee when an attributed order fills. It is not
calculated directly from raw referral volume.

| | Current value |
| --- | ---: |
| Operator taker fee | <OperatorFeeDisplay /> |
| Affiliate share of operator fee | <AffiliateShareDisplay /> |

The trader's total fee also includes the token-specific CLOB base fee. That base fee is
not included when affiliate commission is calculated.

## Claim commission

When a balance is available, select **Claim** in the Affiliate Commissions card and sign
the request. The dashboard updates as referred trades settle and commission becomes
claimable.

<Callout type="info">
  Operator settings are the source of truth for the live rate, attribution rules, and
  payout availability.
</Callout>
