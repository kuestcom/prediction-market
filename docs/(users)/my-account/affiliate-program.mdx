Open **Settings → Affiliate** to copy your referral link and monitor referred activity.

## Dashboard metrics

- referral link and current commission rate;
- total referrals and active traders;
- attributed trading volume;
- earned and claimable affiliate fees.

## How commission works

Commission is recorded when an eligible attributed order fills. It is not calculated
directly from raw referral volume.

## Claim commission

When a balance is available, select **Claim** in the Affiliate Commissions card and sign
the request. The dashboard updates as referred trades settle and commission becomes
claimable.

<Callout type="info">
  Site settings are the source of truth for the live rate, attribution rules, and payout availability.
</Callout>
