---
title: Profile Settings
description: Update the identity shown on your public trader profile
---

Open **Settings** to manage your avatar, email, and username.

## Edit your profile

1. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WEBP avatar up to 5 MB.
2. Update your email or username.
3. Save the changes.
4. Select **View Public Profile** to confirm how the profile appears.

## Username rules

- Usernames must be unique.
- Letters, numbers, periods, and hyphens are accepted.
- A username cannot start or end with a period or hyphen.

<Callout type="info">
  Changing a username changes the handle used in the public profile URL. Update any
  links you have shared.
</Callout>
