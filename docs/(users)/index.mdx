<SiteName /> is a peer-to-peer prediction market. Traders buy and sell outcome shares,
and each share price reflects the market's current estimate of an outcome's probability.

## How a prediction market works

Every prediction is organized into three parts:

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Event** | A topic that groups one or more related questions |
| **Market** | A question you can trade |
| **Outcome** | The side you buy or sell, such as Yes or No |

Outcome shares trade between **$0.00 and $1.00**. A Yes share priced at **$0.37**
represents an implied probability of about **37%**.

## Payouts

When a market reaches its final resolution:

- Winning shares settle to **$1.00** per share.
- Losing shares settle to **$0.00**.
- You can hold until resolution or sell before it ends.

<Callout type="info">
  The market price is not a guarantee. It changes as traders place and fill orders.
</Callout>

## Place your first trade

1. Create your account and connect your wallet.
2. Fund your trading balance.
3. Open a market, choose an outcome, and review the order before signing.
