Your wallet identifies your account and signs trading actions. Creating an account does
not require sharing a seed phrase or private key.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the sign-up flow

    Select **Sign Up** or **Log In** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect your wallet

    Choose a wallet from the connection modal and confirm the requested account.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Authorize the session

    Review and sign the login message. A login signature should not transfer funds.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Complete your profile

    When prompted, choose your username and other account details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before your first trade

<SiteName /> may guide you through a one-time setup for your Deposit Wallet and required
token approvals. Complete each displayed step before submitting an order.

<Callout type="warning" title="Protect your wallet">
  <SiteName /> support will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Verify the site
  address and read every wallet request before signing.
</Callout>
