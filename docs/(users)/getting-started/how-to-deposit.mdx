Open **Deposit** in the header to see the funding methods enabled by your operator.
Availability, supported assets, minimums, and processing time can vary by deployment.

## Funding methods

| Method             | Use it to                                                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wallet**         | Bridge a supported asset from your connected wallet         |
| **Buy Crypto**     | Purchase funds through the integrated card or bank provider |
| **Transfer Funds** | Send USDC on Polygon to the displayed deposit address       |

## Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose a method

    Select Wallet, Buy Crypto, or Transfer Funds in the deposit modal.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify the route

    Confirm the token, network, destination address, expected amount, and provider fee.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Submit

    Approve the wallet transaction or complete the provider flow.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Wait for credit

    Keep the transaction hash or provider reference until the trading balance updates.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Check the token and network">
  Send only the asset and network displayed in the current deposit flow. Unsupported assets or transfers on the wrong
  network may be unrecoverable.
</Callout>

For direct transfers, use the exact USDC token shown by the modal. A test deployment may
show a faucet instead of a production funding route.
