---
title: Claim Your Payout
description: Redeem a winning position after final resolution
---

import { Steps, Step } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps'

You can claim only after the market reaches a final result. Each winning share redeems
for $1.00; losing shares redeem for $0.00.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm final resolution

    Open the market and verify that the timeline shows a final result.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Start the claim

    Use the claim card in **Portfolio** or **Claim winnings** in the resolved market panel.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign and confirm

    Review the included positions, sign the claim request, and wait for confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the balance

    The redeemed amount becomes available for another trade or withdrawal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Your one-time Deposit Wallet and approval setup must be complete before claiming.
</Callout>

For dispute states, payout examples, and troubleshooting, see
[Resolution & Settlement](/docs/resolution-settlement/market-resolution).
