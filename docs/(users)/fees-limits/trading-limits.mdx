title: Limits
description: Review order minimums, balance checks, and provider-specific limits
---

The app enforces order thresholds before signing. Funding providers can apply separate
minimums, maximums, fees, and regional restrictions.

## Trading thresholds

| Rule                | Current app behavior                          |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Market buy minimum  | $1.00                                         |
| Limit order minimum | 0.01 shares                                   |
| Maximum buy         | Available balance and executable liquidity    |
| Maximum sell        | Available shares not committed to open orders |

<Callout type="info">
  A deployment or market can expose additional constraints. The order panel displays the effective minimum and maximum
  for the current trade.
</Callout>

## Deposits and withdrawals

Review the transfer modal for the selected wallet bridge, on-ramp, or transfer route.
Provider limits can vary by asset, network, payment method, account, and region.

## CSV export

**Portfolio → History** exports the rows currently included by your filters. Adjust the
date, type, search, and sort options before downloading the file.
