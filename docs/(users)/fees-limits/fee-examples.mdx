Actual fees are calculated from execution price and filled quantity, not from unfilled
order quantity.

Use the order preview for the estimated dollar amount. A rate parameter should not be
applied as a flat percentage of the entered order amount.

## Partially filled limit order

Suppose a Limit order fills in three separate trades. Each fill is charged from its
execution price and quantity, and the final fee is the sum of those entries. Unfilled
quantity does not incur a fee.

## Affiliate-attributed trade

When an order carries a valid referral, the trader pays the same fee. Eligible affiliate
commission is tracked automatically at the site's current rate.

<Callout type="info">
  Always use the order preview and completed history as the source of truth. Fee
  schedules can vary by market and change over time.
</Callout>
