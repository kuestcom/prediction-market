by the operator. Actual fees are calculated from fills, not from unfilled order quantity.

## Partially filled limit order

Suppose a $300 Limit order fills in three separate trades. Each fill is charged at its
applicable combined rate, and the final fee is the sum of those three entries. Unfilled
quantity does not incur a fee.
