## Expected conduct

- Do not use spam, fraud, impersonation, or deceptive behavior.
- Do not attempt to manipulate support or community processes.
- Provide accurate context and evidence when reporting a market or transaction issue.
- Follow the operator's terms, market rules, and applicable regional restrictions.
- Do not share or request another person's wallet or authentication secrets.

Violations can result in warnings, feature restrictions, suspension, or other action
appropriate to the severity and the operator's terms.

## Report abuse

Use the official support channel and include relevant URLs, public wallet addresses,
transaction hashes, timestamps, and redacted screenshots. Do not confront a suspected
attacker or disclose private credentials.
